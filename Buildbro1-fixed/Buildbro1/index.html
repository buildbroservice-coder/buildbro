ATS stands for Applicant Tracking System. Top recruiters like TCS, Infosys, Cognizant, Wipro, and Accenture use automated bots to scan resumes before a human recruiter reviews them. Resumes made with fancy graphics, multiple columns, tables, or complex Canva icons often scramble the ATS scanner and get automatically rejected. BuildBro formats your resume with clean single-column hierarchy, standard ATS section titles, and keyword optimization to ensure 95%+ parsing success.
Standard delivery for resumes is 24 to 48 hours. If you have an urgent campus drive tomorrow morning, we offer Express Same-Day delivery for just +₹100. For websites, Starter Websites are delivered within 3–5 business days, and Business Websites within 5–7 days.
Every order comes with 2 free rounds of revisions. When you inspect your preview draft, you can ask for adjustments to bullet points, projects, fonts, or details. Additional revision rounds beyond the included two are available at a fair price of ₹50 per revision.
